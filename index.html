Sizing Guide

  1. Place tape across widest part of each nail.
  2. Mark edges, measure in mm, and send us the 10 numbers.
  3. Or order a $5 sizing kit — we’ll mail you blank nails to find your perfect fit (credited to order).
Sizes (mm): 0:18, 1:16.5, 2:15.5, 3:14, 4:13, 5:12, 6:11, 7:10, 8:9, 9:8.
Don’t stress — we include 1 free resize if needed.
